## YouTube channel

I run an educational YouTube channel explaining econometrics, causal inference, and data analysis in accessible, applied ways. Most content is in Spanish and aimed at students and researchers in social sciences.

Topics covered on the channel include:

- **Causal inference**: difference-in-differences, instrumental variables, regression discontinuity
- **Econometrics**: panel data, fixed effects, OLS and its assumptions
- **Data analysis in R and Stata**: practical tutorials with real datasets
- **Research methods for social scientists**: intuition-first explanations before the math
