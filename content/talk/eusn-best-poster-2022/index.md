At the European Conference on Social Networks, I presented a poster on **social interactions and school coexistence in neurodiverse elementary classrooms**. This work later fed into a broader line of research on social integration, reciprocity, and classroom network structure.

The event was especially meaningful because the poster received a **Best Poster award**. I would like this page to eventually hold the poster image, a short explanation of the project, and notes that could later become a LinkedIn post or a public summary.
