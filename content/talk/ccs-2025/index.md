At CCS 2025 I participated with two pieces of work: a presentation on **local and global collective memory** and a poster on **friendship-based inequality in childhood cooperation**.

I like having both kinds of participation recorded in the same place, because conferences often work as milestones for multiple projects at once. Over time this page can grow into a richer record with slides, poster images, photos, and short takeaways.
