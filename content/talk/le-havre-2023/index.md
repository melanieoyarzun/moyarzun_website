At the French Regional Conference on Complex Systems I presented **Unpacking popularity: volume, longevity, connectivity and globality**. This work was part of a broader line of research on collective memory and cultural dynamics, and it helped sharpen how to frame the project for an interdisciplinary complex systems audience.

I also want this section of the website to capture smaller but meaningful conference stops like this one, because they often mark where ideas become clearer and more communicable.
